Golden Harvest Bowl

A colorful mix of roasted vegetables, quinoa, avocado, and a zesty lemon-tahini drizzle. Packed with flavor, fiber, and a naturally vibrant feel.

Serves 2-3

Ingredients

What you need

  • 1 cup quinoa, cooked
  • 1 sweet potato, cubed
  • 1 cup broccoli florets
  • 1 carrot, sliced
  • 1 avocado, sliced
  • 1/2 cup chickpeas, roasted
  • 2 cups baby spinach
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 lemon, juiced
  • 2 tbsp tahini
  • 1 tsp maple syrup
  • Salt and pepper to taste
Method

How to make it

  1. Preheat the oven to 220°C and roast the sweet potato, broccoli, and carrot with olive oil, salt, and pepper for 20–25 minutes.
  2. Whisk together lemon juice, tahini, maple syrup, and a splash of water until the dressing turns smooth and creamy.
  3. Arrange quinoa, spinach, roasted vegetables, avocado, and chickpeas in a large serving bowl or individual bowls.
  4. Drizzle with the lemon-tahini dressing and toss gently before serving.
  5. Top with herbs, pumpkin seeds, or a pinch of chili flakes for extra texture and flavor.

Why it works

Simple ingredients, big flavor

This bowl balances earthy roasted vegetables with creamy avocado and a bright citrus dressing. It is nourishing, easy to customize, and works perfectly for lunches, meal prep, or a light dinner.
